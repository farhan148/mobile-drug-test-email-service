Made-up practice data.

The Hojicha was roasty and cosy, like a campfire in a cup. Best thing in the box.

**2. ★★☆☆☆ (2/5) — Marcus O.**

**4. ★★★★★ (5/5) — Colin B.**

**5. ★★★☆☆ (3/5) — Yasmin T.**
