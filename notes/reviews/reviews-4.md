# Kettlebird tea sampler box: customer reviews (4 of 6)

Made-up practice data.

**1. ★★★★★ (5/5) — Greer N.**
The Jasmine Pearls were gorgeous and fragrant. Rewarding to watch and lovely to drink.

**2. ★★☆☆☆ (2/5) — Omar H.**
Sachets were tiny and the leaflet gave no guidance on how much water. I ended up with a weak cup of Assam.

**3. ★★★★☆ (4/5) — Linnea B.**
Hojicha was my favourite: toasty, mellow, caramel-like. Wanted more of it.

**4. ★★★☆☆ (3/5) — Vikram S.**
Delivery took 12 days. The tea itself was good, particularly the Darjeeling, but I'd have liked it sooner.

**5. ★★★★★ (5/5) — Cora A.**
A beautiful gift. My friend sent a photo of her Chamomile Honey with a thank-you message.

**6. ★☆☆☆☆ (1/5) — Duncan P.**
Opened the box to find a broken tin and loose tea everywhere. The Earl Grey Cream was ruined.

**7. ★★★★☆ (4/5) — Yuki T.**
Sencha and Genmaicha were both excellent. The instructions mixed up the two, which was confusing.

**8. ★★★★★ (5/5) — Ravi M.**
The Masala Chai was perfect with cold weather and a good book. Tasted like proper chai.

**9. ★★★☆☆ (3/5) — Freya D.**
Peach Oolong smelled better than it tasted. Nice, but a little thin.

**10. ★★★★☆ (4/5) — Abel C.**
Good variety. I liked the White Peony most and would have loved a second sachet.

**11. ★★☆☆☆ (2/5) — Noor L.**
Ordered for my mum. The box arrived late and one sachet was open. Disappointing.

**12. ★★★★★ (5/5) — Harriet G.**
Moroccan Mint was refreshing hot and iced. Everyone who tried the box found a favourite.

**13. ★★★☆☆ (3/5) — Seb K.**
The Lapsang Souchong was very smoky. I liked it, my wife did not. A smaller sample would have been enough.

**14. ★★★★☆ (4/5) — Pilar R.**
Rooibos Vanilla was creamy and sweet. Confusing leaflet though, with no steeping time at all for it.

**15. ★★★★★ (5/5) — Jasper O.**
Perfect for trying new teas without committing to a full tin. Tie Guan Yin was a revelation.

**16. ★☆☆☆☆ (1/5) — Maude F.**
Parcel sat at the depot for ten days. The Jasmine Pearls arrived stale-smelling.

**17. ★★★★☆ (4/5) — Anand J.**
Lemon Ginger was bright and zippy. I wanted more, as it was gone in two cups.

**18. ★★★★★ (5/5) — Thea V.**
Brilliant box. Every tea was fresh and fragrant, and the little notes were charming.

**19. ★★★☆☆ (3/5) — Hugo E.**
The teas were tasty, but the box felt flimsy and one corner was crushed on arrival.

**20. ★★★★☆ (4/5) — Selma W.**
Assam Breakfast was brisk and malty. I would have liked one more sachet of the Earl Grey Cream too.
