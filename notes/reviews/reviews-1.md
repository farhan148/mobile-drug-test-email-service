# Kettlebird tea sampler box: customer reviews (1 of 6)

Made-up practice data.

**1. ★★★★★ (5/5) — Hannah P.**
The Darjeeling First Flush was the star. Light, floral, and it kept its flavour on a second steep. Lovely little box.

**2. ★★☆☆☆ (2/5) — Dev R.**
Took 16 days to arrive. The tea was fine, but I bought it as a birthday gift and it came late.

**3. ★★★★☆ (4/5) — Margot L.**
Jasmine Pearls unfurled beautifully in a glass mug. I wish the sachets were bigger, though. One cup and it was gone.

**4. ★★★☆☆ (3/5) — Owen T.**
The Assam Breakfast is strong and malty, just what I like. The card said "steep until ready" which is no help at all.

**5. ★★★★★ (5/5) — Sunita K.**
The Masala Chai smelled like a spice market when I opened the box. I made it with milk on the stove and it was perfect.

**6. ★☆☆☆☆ (1/5) — Brian F.**
The box arrived crushed and two sachets had split. Tea dust all over the other tins. Not a good first impression.

**7. ★★★★☆ (4/5) — Elena V.**
Sencha was grassy and fresh. I scorched it the first time because the water temperature wasn't written down anywhere.

**8. ★★★★★ (5/5) — Callum M.**
Earl Grey Cream is my new evening tea. Soft bergamot, smooth finish. I've already reordered a full tin.

**9. ★★☆☆☆ (2/5) — Priscilla A.**

**10. ★★★★☆ (4/5) — Jonas W.**
Nice variety for the price. Lapsang Souchong was too smoky for me, but my dad loved it.

**11. ★★★★★ (5/5) — Aisha B.**
Gift for my sister and she is already asking where to buy more. The Peach Oolong got the biggest cheer.

**12. ★★★☆☆ (3/5) — Tomasz N.**

**13. ★★★★☆ (4/5) — Ruth E.**

**14. ★★☆☆☆ (2/5) — Kieran D.**

**15. ★★★★★ (5/5) — Mei Ling C.**
