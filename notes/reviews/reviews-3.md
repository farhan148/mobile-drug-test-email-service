# Kettlebird tea sampler box: customer reviews (3 of 6)

Made-up practice data.

**1. ★★★★☆ (4/5) — Ingrid H.**
The Tie Guan Yin was the best oolong I've had in ages: buttery, floral, and it lasted through four steeps.

**2. ★★☆☆☆ (2/5) — Patrick N.**
Brewing instructions were confusing. Some teas said 3 minutes, one said "to taste", and the Sencha said nothing at all.

**3. ★★★★★ (5/5) — Zainab A.**
Beautiful box, beautiful tea. The Darjeeling First Flush was crisp and bright with a muscatel note.

**4. ★★★☆☆ (3/5) — Liam C.**
Good tea, shame about the shipping. A week for the courier to find my street.

**5. ★★★★☆ (4/5) — Carmen D.**
The Peach Oolong was a lovely summer cup, iced. I wish I'd had more than one sachet to try it both ways.

**6. ★★★★★ (5/5) — Felix B.**
Got this on a whim and now I'm a Hojicha convert. Smooth and toasty with no bitterness.

**7. ★☆☆☆☆ (1/5) — Annette P.**
The box was sealed badly and half the sachets were torn. I couldn't use the Moroccan Mint at all.

**8. ★★★★☆ (4/5) — Raj T.**
The Masala Chai needed a long simmer, which the leaflet didn't mention. After that it was excellent.

**9. ★★★★★ (5/5) — Willow M.**
So many lovely smells. Jasmine Pearls and Earl Grey Cream both stood out.

**10. ★★★☆☆ (3/5) — Stuart L.**
Assam Breakfast was strong but flat on its own. Better with a dash of milk.

**11. ★★★★☆ (4/5) — Odette F.**
Chamomile Honey was a sweet bedtime cup. A second sachet would have been nice.

**12. ★★☆☆☆ (2/5) — Keiran O.**
Ordered the sampler as a Christmas gift. It arrived in January.

**13. ★★★★★ (5/5) — Mina S.**
A lovely introduction to loose-leaf. The White Peony gave me a whole new idea of what tea can taste like.

**14. ★★★☆☆ (3/5) — Harvey G.**
Lapsang Souchong was too smoky for me. The Lemon Ginger was a pleasant surprise though.

**15. ★★★★☆ (4/5) — Daria K.**
Sencha was fresh and grassy. Instructions were a little unclear about the water, so I went by guess.

**16. ★★★★★ (5/5) — Joel R.**
Fantastic value for trying so many teas. The Genmaicha was toasty and satisfying.

**17. ★☆☆☆☆ (1/5) — Tessa W.**
Ordered the sampler, got a smaller box than pictured and two teas were missing. No reply to my email.

**18. ★★★★☆ (4/5) — Nikhil V.**
Rooibos Vanilla was smooth and sweet. I had hoped for two cups, but it was only enough for one.

**19. ★★★★★ (5/5) — Poppy E.**
Packaging was pretty, and the teas arrived fresh. Earl Grey Cream is on repeat in our house.

**20. ★★★☆☆ (3/5) — Mateo Z.**
Pleasant range, but it felt pricey for so little tea. The Moroccan Mint was my favourite and it ran out first.
