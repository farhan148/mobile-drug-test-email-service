# Kettlebird tea sampler box: customer reviews (6 of 6)

Made-up practice data.

**1. ★★★★★ (5/5) — Winnie T.**
My favourite was the Peach Oolong, hot and iced. The box was pretty and arrived quickly.

**2. ★★☆☆☆ (2/5) — Lionel S.**
Sachets weren't labelled, so I couldn't tell the Sencha from the Genmaicha. Had to guess from the smell.

**3. ★★★★☆ (4/5) — Dalia R.**
Jasmine Pearls were lovely. The leaflet said "steep gently", which didn't help me much.

**4. ★★★☆☆ (3/5) — Cormac B.**
Assam Breakfast was nice and malty. The box arrived with a crushed lid, but nothing inside was damaged.

**5. ★★★★★ (5/5) — Esme P.**
Chamomile Honey and Rooibos Vanilla were perfect evening teas. I loved the cosy feel of the whole box.

**6. ★☆☆☆☆ (1/5) — Raymond K.**
A month to arrive, and the sachets smelled stale. I would not order again.

**7. ★★★★☆ (4/5) — Tamsin L.**
The White Peony was subtle and sweet. I wanted at least two sachets to really get to know it.

**8. ★★★★★ (5/5) — Ismail G.**
Earl Grey Cream has become my go-to. Smooth and not too perfumed. Ordered a full tin.

**9. ★★★☆☆ (3/5) — Beth N.**
The Moroccan Mint was fresh, but it was so quick to run out. One cup is not much of a taste.

**10. ★★★★☆ (4/5) — Adrian F.**
Tie Guan Yin was beautiful. I did not understand the brewing card, so I brewed it like black tea at first.

**11. ★★★★★ (5/5) — Valentina C.**
Perfect for a lazy weekend. Masala Chai and Hojicha were both warm and comforting.

**12. ★★☆☆☆ (2/5) — Hector D.**
Tracking said delivered, but it hadn't been. It turned up three days later at a neighbour's.

**13. ★★★★☆ (4/5) — Ottilie M.**
Lemon Ginger was a lovely wake-up cup. Just enough for two cups though.

**14. ★★★★★ (5/5) — Santiago A.**
Beautiful gift and lovely tea. Darjeeling First Flush was a highlight.

**15. ★★★☆☆ (3/5) — Pru H.**
The teas were good but nothing blew me away. Lapsang Souchong was the one I'd skip.

**16. ★☆☆☆☆ (1/5) — Clifford W.**
The tin of Genmaicha arrived dented and open. No reply from support when I wrote.

**17. ★★★★☆ (4/5) — Mireille E.**
Sencha was fresh and grassy. A temperature guide on the card would make it perfect.

**18. ★★★★★ (5/5) — Oskar J.**
A warm, thoughtful box and every tea was different. Hojicha and Jasmine Pearls were my picks.

**19. ★★★☆☆ (3/5) — Lena V.**
Good variety, but it felt overpriced for so little tea. Peach Oolong ran out before I was done enjoying it.

**20. ★★★★☆ (4/5) — Kofi B.**
Rooibos Vanilla was lovely with a splash of milk. I'd have liked a second sachet, and quicker shipping.
