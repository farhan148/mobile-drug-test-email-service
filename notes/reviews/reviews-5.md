# Kettlebird tea sampler box: customer reviews (5 of 6)

Made-up practice data.

**1. ★★★★★ (5/5) — Bridget L.**
Everything about this box felt thoughtful. The Darjeeling First Flush was delicate and the Hojicha was cosy.

**2. ★★☆☆☆ (2/5) — Terrence M.**
Three different brewing times were on the box, the leaflet and the sachet. I never knew which to follow.

**3. ★★★★☆ (4/5) — Anya S.**
Genmaicha was lovely and nutty. Wish the box had held a couple more sachets of it.

**4. ★★★☆☆ (3/5) — Dylan R.**
Arrived late for a birthday. The Masala Chai was nice and spicy, which made up for it a bit.

**5. ★★★★★ (5/5) — Phoebe K.**
The Earl Grey Cream was silky and the Jasmine Pearls were stunning. Great present.

**6. ★☆☆☆☆ (1/5) — Walter G.**

**7. ★★★★☆ (4/5) — Lakshmi N.**
Lovely range. The Moroccan Mint was my favourite and the one I wanted most of.

**8. ★★★★★ (5/5) — Callie D.**
A cosy autumn box. Chamomile Honey before bed and Assam with breakfast. I'm hooked.

**9. ★★★☆☆ (3/5) — Everett P.**

**10. ★★★★☆ (4/5) — Rosalind T.**
White Peony was soft and sweet. A bit subtle if you don't pay attention to it.

**11. ★★☆☆☆ (2/5) — Isaac B.**

**12. ★★★★★ (5/5) — Marisol C.**

**13. ★★★☆☆ (3/5) — Nathan V.**

**14. ★★★★☆ (4/5) — Elsie F.**

**15. ★★★★★ (5/5) — Kwame A.**

**18. ★★★★★ (5/5) — Saoirse O.**

**20. ★★★★☆ (4/5) — Hana M.**
