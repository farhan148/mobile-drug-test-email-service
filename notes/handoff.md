# Handoff: Steep Club email 01 subject angle

- **Tea-curious beginners:** want to start without knowing tea. They fear choosing wrong or wasting money.
- **Gift buyers:** want to give a subscription to someone. They worry about arrival time and whether it will be liked.
- **Daily tea drinkers:** want their everyday cup to come from us. They ask why they should switch.
- **Angle A (calm daily ritual):** "slow cup", "quiet minute", "one calm cup a day". All three are 44 characters or fewer.
- **Angle B (discovering new teas):** "try a new tea", "find your next cup", "explore new teas". All three are 44 characters or fewer.
- **Pick: Angle A, subject 1** ("Your Steep Club spot is ready for a slow cup"). It keeps the waitlist spot and suits all three groups.
- **Why not B:** it hints at variety, but we do not know what is in the box yet. It also fits daily drinkers poorly.
- **Caveat:** A leans on the "Calm" voice word, which the owner has not confirmed. Neither angle speaks to gift buyers' worries.
