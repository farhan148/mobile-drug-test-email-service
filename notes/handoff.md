# Handoff: email 01 subject angles (Book a Test)

- **Employers:** keeping the workplace running, with no staff trip to a clinic.
- **Individuals:** a calm, simple, private process. We never say why they test.
- **Existing clients:** being recognised, and an easy repeat or program booking.
- **Angle A (save time, stay compliant):** workplace-focused lines like "Save a trip: on-site workplace testing". Suits employers best. The "stay on top of" line hints at a routine, and the owner has not confirmed any compliance claim.
- **Angle B (fast, convenient):** "A technician comes to you", "On-site testing, no clinic trip", "Book an on-site test in one link". Convenience only, no speed claims.
- **Pick: Angle B.** It fits all three groups, while A fits only employers. It also avoids anything that sounds like a legal outcome or a turnaround time.
- **Suggested subject:** "A technician comes to you" (25 characters). It is already used in all three group versions of email 01.
