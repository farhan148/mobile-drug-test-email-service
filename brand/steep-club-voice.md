## About the brand

The Steep Club is a tea subscription. Our emails go to people on the waitlist, in three groups: tea-curious beginners, gift buyers, and daily tea drinkers. The first goal is to help them start a subscription.

## Our voice in three words

1. **Clear.** Short sentences, plain English, no jargon. (Confirmed.)
2. **Friendly.** We write like a tea-loving friend, not a shop assistant. **Confirm.**
3. **Calm.** No pressure, no countdown panic. Tea is a slow, pleasant thing. **Confirm.**

## Words we use

- tea, cup, brew, steep
- subscription, delivery, box
- waitlist, your spot
- beginner-friendly, gift

Use "you" and "your". Use short, everyday words. Say what is in the box and what it costs, in plain terms.

**Confirm:** any special brand words, such as "the Club" or "members".

## Words we avoid

- Hype words: "revolutionary", "game-changer", "ultimate", "life-changing".
- Fake urgency, such as "Hurry!" or "Last chance!!!". Only state a deadline if it is real.
- Tea snob talk and jargon that beginners will not know. If we must use a tea term, explain it.
- Long, complicated sentences.

## Claims we must never make

- No health claims. Never say tea detoxes, cleanses, boosts the immune system, helps you lose weight, or treats or prevents anything.
- No promises about savings, prices, or discounts unless the offer is confirmed and stated exactly.
- No promises about taste, such as "you will love it". Say what is in the box instead.
- No claims about where the tea comes from, how it is grown, or how it is certified (organic, fair trade, and so on) unless we can prove it.
- No claims about delivery dates unless they are confirmed.
- Never promise any other result we cannot prove.

Talk about what the box contains, how to start, how to pause or cancel, and how gifting works.
